GROWTH HUB WEBSITE
==================

Files:
- index.html  — complete responsive website
- style.css   — Growth Hub dark blue / green theme
- script.js   — mobile menu, scroll animations, active nav and demo form
- assets/logo.png     — supplied Growth Hub logo
- assets/founder.jpg  — supplied founder photo

Open index.html in a browser to preview.

The contact form is front-end only. Connect it to Formspree, Netlify Forms, your backend, or a CRM before publishing if you want submissions to be delivered.
